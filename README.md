# 🏬 Decathlon Retail Performance Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

**An Interactive Business Intelligence Solution for Retail Sales, Profitability & Performance Analysis**

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Decathlon Retail Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze retail business performance and transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The primary objective of this project is to help business stakeholders understand sales trends, evaluate product and store performance, monitor profitability, and compare current business performance with previous-year results.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, comparisons, and filters, helping users explore business performance and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard focuses on answering important business questions:

- How is the business performing in terms of net sales and profitability?
- How do current Year-to-Date (YTD) sales compare with Previous Year-to-Date (PYTD) sales?
- Which products contribute the most to overall sales?
- Which stores perform better and which require attention?
- How is the average order value changing over time?
- What are the major trends and patterns in retail performance?
- Where can the business identify opportunities for improvement?

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard focuses on important retail performance metrics:

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Net Sales | Measures revenue generated after applicable deductions |
| Gross Profit | Evaluates profit generated from sales |
| Profit Margin | Measures profitability as a percentage of sales |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Measures the average value of customer orders |
| YTD Sales | Tracks sales performance from the beginning of the year |
| PYTD Sales | Measures sales for the equivalent previous-year period |
| YTD vs PYTD Growth | Highlights changes in performance compared with the previous year |

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Analysis

### 1. Net Sales Analysis
- Monitors overall net sales performance.
- Compares YTD and PYTD sales.
- Highlights sales growth and decline.
- Helps identify important sales trends.

### 2. Product Performance Analysis
- Evaluates product-level sales performance.
- Identifies high-performing and low-performing products.
- Helps understand product contribution to overall business performance.

### 3. Store Performance Analysis
- Compares performance across different stores.
- Highlights differences in store-level sales.
- Helps identify stores performing well and stores that may need improvement.

### 4. Profitability Analysis
- Tracks gross profit and profit margin.
- Helps evaluate business profitability.
- Supports better understanding of revenue and profit performance.

### 5. Average Order Value Analysis
- Tracks average order value.
- Helps understand changes in customer purchasing value.
- Provides another perspective on retail sales performance.
